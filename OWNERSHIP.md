retains ownership of the SHINE name, brand, and trademark rights.

housing operations under the terms of the SHINE handoff agreement. That
license does not give DISH ownership of SHINE.

Any resale, sublicensing, or provision of SHINE to another organization must
be approved in writing by Race Anderson.
