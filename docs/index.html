Supportive Housing Incident Navigation Engine

Voice-first incident reporting for supportive housing communities.

SHINE Voice Report helps residents report concerns by phone or QR code. A calm Vapi voice assistant gathers the details, then management receives a structured incident report by email.

Safety first

SHINE is for incident reporting workflows, not emergency dispatch. If there is immediate danger, call 911 first.

Overview

A lower-barrier way to report concerns.

SHINE is an open-source workflow for agencies that want residents to report concerns without filling out paper forms or navigating complex portals. Residents speak naturally, and the system turns the call into a written report for staff follow-up.

Voice assistant

Residents call a number or scan a flyer QR code to reach Vapi.

Webhook backend

A Node/Express backend receives end-of-call reports from Vapi.

Email reports

Management receives summaries, transcripts, and structured fields.

How it works

From resident phone call to staff-ready report.

  1. 1

    Resident starts a call

    They call the published phone number or scan a QR code on a flyer.

  2. 2

    Vapi asks calm questions

    The assistant gathers key details about what happened and where.

  3. 3

    Backend receives the webhook

    When the call ends, Vapi sends the summary, transcript, and fields.

  4. 4

    Management gets an email

    Staff receive a structured incident report for review and follow-up.
